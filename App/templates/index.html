Ⅰ. The Knowledge Ethos

MindVault exists because "if a student has resources, then he can understand." All books, peer-reviewed papers, and notes are curated strictly for non-commercial student empowerment.

Ⅱ. Academic Integrity & Attribution

Readers are granted unrestricted digital access for study and research. Intellectual property remains with original authors; citations must honor academic attribution.

Users must submit authentic feedback and adhere to respectful discourse. Administrators reserve the right to review materials to maintain archival purity.
